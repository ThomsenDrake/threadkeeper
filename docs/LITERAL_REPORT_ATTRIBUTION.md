# Literal report-attribution completeness

[PR #21](https://github.com/ThomsenDrake/threadkeeper/pull/21) is explicitly stacked on the assertion-kind work in PR #19, starting at `81c1159c5694e70434aadbe9bfa0c777380ec95a` and integrating its final evidence/docs head `7348c7e3a88f01260a0dbfd34e4b001789b5da67`. The separate seven-case live probe lost the named reporter from one statement and quote while correctly preserving structured `agent_reported` provenance. Its strict **4/7** score and original evidence remain unchanged. This guard addresses only a literal loss of reporting context; it does not fix general extraction quality, omitted assertions or kind classification.

The [predeclared synthetic controls](../tests/fixtures/literal-report-controls.ts) use unrelated reporter names and assertions. They were frozen before implementation at `edde7bb`, with peer-requested boundary controls added at `0a44643`. The guard raises a sanitized `extraction_missing_report_attribution` error through the existing maximum of two extraction attempts. Code does not fill attribution, change kind/origin/status or rewrite either statement or source evidence.

The implemented envelope is deliberately syntactic, not a semantic proof that a statement is positive or true:

- Original source role is `assistant`, source origin and normalized extracted origin are both `agent_reported`.
- The complete trimmed source is one line: a bounded reporter followed by literal ` reports: "`, one inner assertion, its closing ASCII double quote, and optionally one final period. Exactly one quote pair is allowed; surrounding context is outside scope.
- A reporter is either one to three capitalized ASCII name tokens or `The` plus one/two ASCII descriptor tokens and a role noun (`agent`, `monitor`, `worker`, `service`, `reviewer`, `tool`). Reporter text is at most 80 characters, with single spaces. Pronouns, indefinite/unnamed speakers and detected negative, hypothetical or compound cues are excluded. This recognizes a limited name/description shape, not real identity.
- The inner assertion is at most 4,000 characters. After trimming and removing one trailing period, only ASCII letters, digits, spaces and hyphens are allowed. Detected negative, hypothetical, compound and nested-report cues are excluded. Non-ASCII text, apostrophes, abbreviations, extra sentences, other punctuation and ambiguous cases are deliberately left unchanged.
- The extracted statement must equal that entire inner assertion after only trimming and removing one terminal period. The exact evidence quote must equal either the complete inner assertion or the complete source after trimming. An arbitrary partial quote cannot establish that the same complete assertion lost its reporting context.

Controlled responses verify successful second-attempt repair with retained attributed text and unchanged source input, and rejection of the entire extraction after two omissions, including a batch with another valid memory. Already attributed statements and all predeclared ambiguity/source/paraphrase controls remain unchanged. The guard does not generally validate reporter names, paraphrases or model-supplied attribution. It does not guarantee report retention or a successful repair: an empty result or other paraphrase is outside this literal omission check. Source-owned origin checks and existing evidence validation remain authoritative.
